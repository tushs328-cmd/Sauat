# BUG-002 — интерактивные цели меньше 44 px

- Severity: P2
- Status: reproduced
- Scope: W1, RU/KK, synthetic browser simulation (`СИМУЛЯЦИЯ`)

## Steps

1. Open the map at 360×800 or complete a story task.
2. Measure visible `button`, `a`, and role-button bounding boxes.

## Expected

Interactive touch targets are at least 44×44 px, including secondary controls.

## Actual

The current audit records, among others: story node `40×40`, «Результаты»/«Нәтижелер» `332×37` or `88×37`, «Сбросить прогресс»/«Прогресті тазарту» `111×32` or `104×32`, task back controls `30×44` and `76–89×33`, and the skip link height `21` px.
